## How to sign

On your first pull request, a bot posts a comment linking to this document.
Reply on the pull request with:

> I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA

The bot records your GitHub username, the date and the pull request in the
`cla-signatures` branch of this repository. Signing once covers all your
later contributions.
